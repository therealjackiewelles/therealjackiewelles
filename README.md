## Hi, I'm Morgan 👋

🔊 I'm a sound engineer based in Gloucestershire, England, working as a **Systems Engineer, PA Tech and FoH Engineer**. I freelance with SSE Audio, flying PA systems and looking after audio networking for large-scale arena and stadium tours.

🌐 My day-to-day work covers PA deployment, system configuration and the networked audio infrastructure behind the show. That includes AVB/Milan networks, gPTP clocking, L-Acoustics systems and managed fibre switching. Alongside touring, I build software that gives show engineers a clearer view of the systems they're responsible for, using tools designed around how a live rig actually runs.

|---|---|---|
| 📡 **[Mping](https://github.com/therealjackiewelles/Mping)** | `Swift` `SwiftUI` `macOS` | Network monitoring built for show engineers, not IT departments. Your rig is shown as a live topology map, with RTT, jitter, packet loss, per-end fibre loss and SFP optics read over DDM. A device is never marked offline until a verification burst confirms it, so there are no false alerts. Built around Netgear AV, AVB/Milan and L-Acoustics rigs. Currently in beta with engineers on tour. |
| 🌡️ **[UnDelayUnDelay](https://github.com/therealjackiewelles/UnDelayUnDelay)** | `macOS` | A small floating card for timed PA systems. You enter the temperature and humidity the rig was timed at and the current conditions. It then shows how far the speed of sound has shifted your alignment, in milliseconds and in samples at 44.1, 48 and 96 kHz. |
| 🎛️ **[DirectOut Companion module](https://github.com/therealjackiewelles/companion-module-directout-audioprocessors)** | `TypeScript` `Node.js` `Bitfocus Companion` | A fork of the DirectOut PRODIGY/MAVEN module for Companion. It adds offline snapshots, so you can build control pages with your named channels without a device connected (from a hotel room, say), and a fader-curve step mode for rotary encoders. |
